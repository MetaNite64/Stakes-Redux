# Stakes Redux
A mod that completely overhauls nearly all of the Stakes in Balatro for a more fun experience.

## Stakes
- new Pink Stake: skipping blinds also skips the shop. This is actually a change in behavior for White Stake, allowing the player to visit the shop after skipping a blind.
- Red Stake: all blind rewards are $1 less
- Orange Stake: Editions are twice as rare
- new Yellow Stake: Interest is earned at a rate of $1 for every $6 owned
- Green Stake: faster ante scaling (same as vanilla Green Stake)
- new Cyan Stake: hand level ups are 80% as effective
- Blue Stake: When cards are discarded, one less card is drawn
- Purple Stake: Showdown Blinds are encountered every 4 Antes
- Black Stake: even faster ante scaling (same as vanilla Purple Stake)
- new Sticker Stakes, that apply the Eternal, Perishable, Rental, and new Gigantic, Blighted, and Traitorous stickers on top of White Stake and other Sticker Stakes only
- Gold Stake: X1.25 shop costs, applies both Black Stake and all the Sticker Stakes
- new Platinum Stake: stickers may appear on playing cards in the shop

## Stickers
Stickers can optionally be moved to a separate RunSelect page, completely independent of your Stake selection. Want to play with just Rental stickers on White Stake? Only the new stickers that Stakes Redux adds on Yellow Stake? Gold Stake with no stickers at all? Go for it!

New stickers:
- Gigantic: Takes up 2 Joker slots
- Blighted: When this Joker triggers, a random playing card loses 4 Chips. Cards self-destruct once they reach 0 chips
- Traitorous: After a hand is played, gain X0.2 Blind size per card held in hand

## Requirements
This mod requires the [latest version of SMODS](https://github.com/Steamodded/smods/releases/latest), `26.829.0`.
