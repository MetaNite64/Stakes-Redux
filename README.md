# Stakes Redux
A mod that completely overhauls nearly all of the Stakes in Balatro for a more fun experience.
Stakes include:
- new Pink Stake: skipping blinds also skips the shop. This is actually a change in behavior for White Stake, allowing the player to visit the shop after skipping a blind.
- Red Stake: all blind rewards are $1 less
- Orange Stake: Editions are twice as rare
- new Yellow Stake: Interest is earned at a rate of $1 for every $6 owned
- Green Stake: faster ante scaling (same as vanilla Green Stake)
- Blue Stake: hand level ups are 80% as effective
- Purple Stake: Showdown Blinds are encountered every 4 Antes
- Black Stake: even faster ante scaling (same as vanilla Purple Stake)
- new Sticker Stakes, that apply the Eternal, Perishable, Rental, and new Gigantic stickers on top of White Stake and other Sticker Stakes only
- Gold Stake: X1.25 shop costs, applies both Black Stake and all the Sticker Stakes
- new Platinum Stake: stickers may appear on playing cards in the shop

## Requirements
This mod requires the [latest version of SMODS](https://github.com/Steamodded/smods/releases/latest), `1.0.0-beta-1531zeebee`.
